Your First Visit in 3 Steps

Most new patients are in and out in under an hour, and leave with relief and a written plan.

  1. Before you arrive · 15 min

    Digital intake

    Fill in your history and insurance from your phone, so the visit starts with you, not a clipboard.

  2. Visit one · about 45 min

    Targeted exam & adjustment
